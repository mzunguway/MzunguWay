Acquisition FAQ

Clear before you click.

The essentials about pricing, offers, what is included and how ownership changes hands.

Ask another question →
Where can I see the acquisition price?+

Current acquisition terms are shown on the active marketplace listing when one is available, or discussed through a private enquiry. MzunguWay intentionally does not publish prices on this website.

What exactly is included?+

The acquisition covers the domain name only unless a separate written agreement explicitly states otherwise. The concepts shown on MzunguWay are illustrative ideas, not an existing website, business, traffic guarantee or trademark right.

Can I submit a private offer?+

Yes. Choose “Make a private offer” on the relevant domain card. The message will already identify the domain and leave space for your company, proposal and timeline.

How is the transaction completed?+

Use the active marketplace route shown for the domain when available. Where no marketplace route is displayed, contact MzunguWay first to confirm the transaction process and current terms.

Can I acquire a bundle of domains?+

Yes. Use “Request Bundle Offer” for a curated pair. We will confirm availability of every included domain, combined pricing and transaction and transfer terms. Individual marketplace listings are separate from a bundle enquiry. No package price or discount is implied, and individual enquiries remain welcome.
