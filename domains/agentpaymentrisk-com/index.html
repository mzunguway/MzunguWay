The Story

The shift is already here.

Agentic commerce went from concept to checkout in under two years. Major AI and payment platforms are building agent-native payment rails. But payment volume without risk infrastructure is how losses happen — velocity attacks, runaway agents, delegated fraud. Risk officers are now writing agent policies into procurement requirements. The vocabulary they use? Agent payment risk. One domain captures the exact moment a category gets its name.
