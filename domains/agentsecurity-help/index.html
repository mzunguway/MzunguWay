The Story

The numbers tell the story.

Industry reports now attribute a growing share of AI incidents to agent misbehavior — runaway automation, prompt-based manipulation, unauthorized data access. Regulators on both sides of the Atlantic are drafting agent governance rules as you read this. Every new deployment creates demand for practical, incident-ready guidance. A domain that ends in “.help” isn't branding — it's the instinct of every engineer at 2 AM.

Why this domain

The opportunity

Composition. “Agent security” states the problem domain; “.help” states what the visitor expects to find when something goes wrong.

  • Phonetics. Two common words, immediately understandable in incident calls, tickets and internal escalation messages.
  • Quiet advantage. The extension carries intent. It feels like a destination to use under pressure, not a marketplace to browse.
