Why this domain

The opportunity

The .help extension turns a complex threat into a direct promise of assistance. The name suits a high-trust destination for people and organizations facing synthetic-media abuse.

  • The subject and the purpose sit on either side of the dot: deepfakes, and help dealing with them.
  • The name is broad enough for synthetic images, video and audio, without tying the service to one detection technology.

When this name is the right fit

Choose it for an educational or first-response resource that makes reporting routes and qualified assistance easier to find.

The naming trade-off

A help-oriented address creates expectations of responsible guidance. It is not evidence of official status, guaranteed detection or successful content removal.

The problem

When a person or company discovers suspected synthetic audio, video or imagery, the first challenge is knowing what to preserve, how to verify it, where to report it and who can help before evidence disappears or harm spreads.

What someone could build

Imagine a trusted first-response portal that guides evidence preservation, accepts suspicious media for authenticity triage, generates reporting checklists, routes users toward platform takedown procedures and connects serious cases to cybersecurity or legal specialists.

How the concept could work

  1. Choose the incident type: video, image, audio or impersonation.
  2. Preserve URLs, files, timestamps, screenshots and context.
  3. Submit material for technical triage or expert review.
  4. Receive platform-specific reporting and takedown guidance.
  5. Escalate high-risk cases to legal, fraud or cybersecurity support.

A focused first launch

Publish clear routes for individuals and organizations, with evidence-preservation guidance and links to current platform reporting procedures.
