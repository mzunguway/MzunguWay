The Story

The infrastructure moment is now.

FedNow adoption is accelerating across US banks, instant schemes are live in 70+ countries, and regulators are pushing real-time settlement as standard. But most institutions can't replace core banking systems overnight — they bolt on. The winning architecture in every rollout is the same: not a replacement, a layer. The domain says exactly where the product sits. The name is the sales deck.

How the concept could work

  1. Connect the bank core and customer channels.
  2. Add one or more instant-payment schemes.
  3. Normalize messages, states and error handling.
  4. Apply routing, limits and operational controls.
  5. Expose monitoring and reconciliation to operations teams.

A focused first launch

Start with one scheme, one core-banking connector and a transparent API contract. Prove the layer before expanding the rail map.
