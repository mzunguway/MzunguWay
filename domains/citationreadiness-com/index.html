Citation Readiness Audit for AI Search & Answer Engines

The problem

Marketing teams can publish excellent content that is still difficult for search and answer systems to interpret because facts are buried, entities are unclear, sources are weak or page structure is inconsistent.

What someone could build

Imagine a B2B audit platform that scans a site and scores factual clarity, sources, entities, authorship, freshness, structured data and machine-readable information. It would identify what makes content easier to understand and reference without promising citations.

How the concept could work

  1. Crawl the selected website or content library.
  2. Score factual clarity, sourcing, structure, entities and freshness.
  3. Detect missing or weak structured-data and authority signals.
  4. Generate a prioritized Citation Readiness action plan.
  5. Re-scan and track readiness improvements over time.
