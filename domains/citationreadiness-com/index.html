Market context — 2026

What citation readiness means

The concept comes first.

Citation readiness is not a promise that an AI system will quote or rank a page. It is a practical way to assess whether a site presents facts, entities, sources, authorship, freshness and structured information clearly enough to be understood and referenced.

  • It can become a repeatable audit method for agencies and software platforms.
  • It turns a vague “AI visibility” goal into a set of observable content and technical checks.
  • It creates a language for prioritizing improvements without claiming guaranteed citations.
