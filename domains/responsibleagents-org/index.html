Why this domain

The opportunity

The phrase is broad enough to convene an ecosystem yet precise enough to own a clear mission. The .org extension supports a standards initiative, nonprofit, research coalition or industry consortium.

  • Responsible agents articulates a mission rather than promising a particular technical mechanism.
  • The .org address can suit a shared research or governance initiative involving several organizations.
