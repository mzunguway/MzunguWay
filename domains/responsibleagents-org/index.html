The Story

The regulatory clock is running.

The EU AI Act is entering its enforcement phases, the US has issued agent governance guidance, and sector regulators are drafting their own rules for autonomous systems. Every compliance framework will need implementing bodies — consortia, standards groups, certification programs. The extension for that work is not “.com”. It's “.org”: the historical home of charters, coalitions and public trust.

Market context — 2026

Why this domain

The opportunity

Composition. “Responsible agents” names the mission rather than a single technical control; “.org” reinforces institutional and collaborative positioning.

  • Phonetics. Two ordinary words, calm and credible in policy, academic and standards settings.
  • Quiet advantage. The name can host a charter, working group or certification initiative without sounding like a commercial product trying to borrow institutional authority.

When this name is the right fit

Choose it for a consortium, nonprofit, academic program, standards initiative or certification body focused on accountable agent deployment.
