The Story

The threat has a name, and the industry voted on it.

Prompt injection ranks #1 on the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications — above data leaks, above supply chain attacks. Why? Because it's trivially easy: no exploit kit, no malware, just carefully worded text. Every company shipping an LLM feature inherits this problem on day one. Security teams search the exact term. This domain is what they find.
