Hadithi

Malipo ya agent si bidhaa moja, ni mnyororo wa uaminifu. agentpaymentid.com inaweka identity ya actor, agentpaymentrisk.com inaongeza risk layer, InstantPayLayer.com inabeba execution ya papo hapo, na KycHuman.com inamrudisha human reviewer pale automation inapofikia mpaka wake. Majina manne, layers nne, architecture moja.
