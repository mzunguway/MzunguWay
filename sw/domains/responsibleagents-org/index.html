Kwa nini jina hili

“Responsible agents” linaeleza mission badala ya control moja ya kiufundi, huku “.org” likiunga mkono uelekeo wa taasisi, research na collaboration.

Jina hili linaweza kumfaa nani

Industry consortia, nonprofits, universities, research institutes, standards bodies na certification programs.

Unachoweza kujenga

Mpango huru unaochapisha agent-governance principles, templates, evaluation criteria, research na case studies, kisha kuwaleta pamoja watafiti, regulators, developer na enterprises.

Matumizi

Governance research · Policy templates · Industry collaboration · Certification criteria
