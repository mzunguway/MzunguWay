The Story

Four layers, one payment architecture.

Agentic payments are not one product. They are a chain of trust. First the actor needs an identity with agentpaymentid.com. Then each transaction needs a risk layer with agentpaymentrisk.com. Execution moves in real time through InstantPayLayer.com. And when automation reaches its limit, KycHuman.com brings the human reviewer back into the loop.

Domains in this bundle
  • agentpaymentid.com — identity
  • agentpaymentrisk.com — risk
  • InstantPayLayer.com — instant execution
  • KycHuman.com — human verification
Strategic coverage
